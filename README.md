# Creative Strategy OS

**From customer and market evidence to clear creative direction.**

Creative Strategy OS turns brand context, customer language, competitor signals, and advertising data into differentiated creative concepts, production-ready briefs, and approved Meta copy.

**Built by:** [Fahida Cécile](https://github.com/fahidacecile), Growth Marketer & BI Analyst

## The problem

Creative teams rarely lack ideas. They lack a reliable way to connect customer evidence, competitive context, creative diversity, and production decisions.

Research is often scattered across review platforms, ad libraries, documents, and dashboards. Creative Strategy OS brings those inputs into one structured workflow so that each recommendation can be traced back to real evidence.

## What the system does

- Structures brand, product, market, channel, and business objectives in one project brief.
- Organizes research from brand websites, advertising libraries, independent reviews, and customer conversations.
- Identifies three strategic creative gaps: **persona, awareness, and diversity**.
- Maps differentiated concepts through an Andromeda-aligned creative grid.
- Converts approved concepts into production-ready briefs and paid-social copy.
- Applies sequential quality control before any deliverable is considered complete.
- Packages research, strategy, creative concepts, copy, and reporting into reusable outputs.

## Operating workflow

| Stage | Purpose | Key output |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **01 · Evidence** | Establish brand, customer, category, and competitor truth | Research Masterfile |
| **02 · Diagnosis** | Find underused personas, awareness levels, and creative territories | Three Creative Gaps |
| **03 · Strategy** | Build distinct concepts across messages, formats, and audience states | Andromeda Grid |
| **04 · Quality** | Review briefs and copy through sequential specialist checks | Approved Creative Briefs |
| **05 · Delivery** | Package decisions into assets that teams can use immediately | Copy Pack, Roadmap, PPTX |

## Two analysis modes

### Full Creative Strategy

A complete research-to-production workflow designed to produce:

- Research Masterfile
- Brand and market diagnosis
- Persona and awareness mapping
- Competitive creative analysis
- Andromeda concept grid
- Production-ready creative briefs
- Meta Copy Pack
- Quality-control report

### Creative Gap Audit

A focused diagnostic designed to reveal:

- Reputation and customer-language signals
- Dominant and underrepresented personas
- Awareness-stage coverage
- Creative diversity gaps
- Priority concepts and testing opportunities
- Actionable roadmap
- Executive PowerPoint report

## Evidence inputs

The methodology is designed to work with evidence from:

- Official brand and product pages
- Meta Ad Library
- G2 and Capterra
- Trustpilot
- Reddit and customer communities
- YouTube and TikTok
- Competitor websites and advertising
- Structured collections of customer verbatims

Brand-owned testimonials are treated separately from independent customer evidence.

## Quality gates

Creative Strategy OS does not treat generation as completion.

- **Andromeda Batch Score:** concepts must reach **75/100 or higher** before copy is written.
- **Eight-agent review:** specialist reviewers assess strategy, platform fit, persuasion, clarity, brand alignment, evidence, and production readiness.
- **Review threshold:** Agents 1–7 must reach **90/100 or higher**.
- **Final veto:** Agent 8 can approve or block the deliverable.

These gates are designed to prevent repetitive concepts, unsupported claims, weak briefs, and production ambiguity.

## Product principles

1. **Evidence before ideas** — recommendations begin with observable customer and market signals.
2. **Diversity before volume** — a batch should explore meaningfully different territories, not cosmetic variations.
3. **Quality before delivery** — every output must pass explicit strategic and production checks.
4. **Traceability over black-box generation** — major decisions should remain connected to their supporting evidence.
5. **Useful outputs over impressive documents** — deliverables must help a team decide, produce, test, and learn.
