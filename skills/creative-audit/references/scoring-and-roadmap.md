# Scoring, concepts, and roadmap

Use this reference after the evidence and gap diagnosis are complete.

## Andromeda-aligned diversity score

Use this as a secondary diagnostic for the creative-diversity gap. It is a planning heuristic, not an official platform guarantee.

| Dimension | Points | Scoring |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Concept volume | 15 | 15 for 12+; 10 for 10-11; 5 for 7-9; 0 below 7 |
| Creative similarity | 20 | Use platform-native data when available. Otherwise mark `not measured` and report only qualitative clusters. |
| Hook diversity | 20 | 20 for 4+ archetypes; 12 for 3; 5 for 2; 0 for 1 |
| Format mix | 20 | 20 for a strategically justified multi-format mix; 12 for two strong formats; 5 for narrow coverage; 0 for one repeated format |
| Persona coverage | 15 | 15 for 3+ personas with 2+ concepts each; 10 for broad coverage; 5 when one persona exceeds half the sample; 0 for one persona only |
| Angle spread | 10 | 10 for 3+ meaningful angles with no unjustified dominance; 6 for partial spread; 3 for one dominant angle; 0 for one angle |

If all dimensions are measurable:

- 75-100: well supplied
- 50-74: under-diversified, with visible near-term opportunities
- 0-49: highly concentrated

If a dimension is not measurable, show the measured subtotal and label the total `provisional, incomplete`. Do not rescale it.

## Scorecards

Score each 1-10 with written evidence and limitations:

- Creative strength
- Messaging clarity
- Offer communication
- Funnel coherence
- Evidence and proof
- Andromeda readiness

These are structured audit judgments, not performance metrics.

## Gap-led concepts

For each of three to five concepts include:

- Named gap closed
- Persona
- Awareness and funnel job
- Human tension
- Strategic angle
- Creator or production representation
- Hook direction
- Proof path
- Claim boundary
- Expected learning
- Evidence IDs
- Production complexity: low, medium, or high

Rank concepts by evidence strength, distinct audience value, differentiation, proof availability, production feasibility, and learning value.

## Roadmap

### Days 0-30: establish the learning baseline

- Resolve evidence gaps
- Produce the first concepts tied to the highest-confidence opportunity
- Define controls, variables, and decision rules
- Fix tracking or naming needed for clean comparison

### Days 31-60: expand the strongest territory

- Iterate a validated angle across personas, hooks, creators, or formats
- Add the missing awareness job
- Address the strongest objection with an appropriate proof mechanism

### Days 61-90: build portfolio resilience

- Launch the next net-new audience or territory
- Expand creator and partnership representation where relevant
- Feed results back into the concept matrix
- Retire unsupported assumptions and document learnings

For each roadmap item include owner, input, output, launch window, hypothesis, metric, decision rule, and next action.
