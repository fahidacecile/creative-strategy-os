# Evidence collection and gap diagnosis

Use this reference during the Creative Audit.

## Reputation and brand context

Recreate the research path of a potential buyer:

- Founding story and stated reason for the brand
- Hero products, merchandising, pricing, offers, guarantees, bundles, and likely paid-social landing pages
- Product promise, mechanism, features, benefits, proof, and claim boundaries
- First-page search reputation and visible friction
- Earned press versus sponsored or paid placement
- Material launches, retail expansion, partnerships, or category events
- Organic content, community language, creator mix, and employee or founder presence

Do not infer unit economics from price alone. Frame bundle or AOV ideas as hypotheses that require internal margin, conversion, and repeat-purchase data.

## Independent customer evidence

Use independent reviews, forums, Reddit, retail reviews, G2, Capterra, Trustpilot, and public video or social comments as relevant to the category. Exclude brand-site testimonials from the independent evidence set.

Capture:

- Pre-purchase frustration
- Desired progress and transformation language
- Specific outcomes, with claim caution
- Objections and reasons for hesitation
- Identity and use-case language
- Competitor comparisons
- Recurring questions
- Three to five short `golden nugget` excerpts with source URLs

For each insight record evidence IDs, volume, emotional intensity, contradictions, and confidence. Respect quotation limits and avoid collecting personally identifiable information.

## Personas or jobs-to-be-done

Build three to five evidence-backed profiles. For each include situation, desired progress, pain, trigger, objection, proof required, awareness state, supporting excerpts, volume, intensity, and confidence.

Do not infer sensitive traits or demographics from names, photos, or stereotypes.

## Visible advertising sample

For the target brand, capture up to 100 accessible active ads and ads stopped within the last 90 days. For a larger library, balance the sample by age, status, persona, angle, format, and destination.

| Field | Capture |
| --- | --- |
| Ad ID and URL | Stable reference to the observed ad |
| Observed date | When the audit saw it |
| Visible status | Active or inactive when shown |
| First-seen date | Only when the library provides it |
| Persona | Who is represented or addressed, with rationale |
| Awareness and funnel job | Unaware to most aware; TOFU, MOFU, or BOFU |
| Angle | Clinical proof, peer, founder, routine, education, comparison, offer, or another observed angle |
| Hook | Confession, claim, curiosity, relatability, before/after, question, statistic, or another observed archetype |
| Format | Static, video, carousel, plus useful micro-format |
| Proof and objection | Proof shown and objection answered |
| Creator signal | Creator type and partnership label if visible |
| CTA and destination | Exact visible CTA and landing destination |
| Similarity note | Qualitative cluster only unless platform data is available |

Summarize:

- Personas currently represented
- Estimated awareness mix with counts and percentages
- Angles per persona
- Hook and proof diversity
- Macro and micro format mix
- Creator representation
- Partnership-ad presence
- Similarity clusters

## Direct competitors

Select five direct competitors using product use case, customer, price or business model, geography, and actual buying alternative. Keep adjacent category inspirations separate.

For each competitor, capture the same observable fields at a proportionate sample size. Compare signals, not presumed performance.

## Gap diagnosis

### Persona gap

Cross real customer profiles with people represented or addressed in current ads. A strong gap requires a material customer signal and little or no visible representation. State:

`Current ads primarily address [A]. Independent evidence also shows [B], which is not observed in the visible sample. Testing [B] could reach a distinct buying context.`

### Awareness gap

Estimate the current mix across awareness and funnel jobs. Identify the missing job required to connect discovery, consideration, and conversion.

### Creative-diversity gap

Look for repeated angles, hooks, formats, proof, settings, creators, and visual systems. Distinguish iteration from net-new concepts.

### Proof or objection gap

Map repeated customer objections to proof actually visible in ads. Identify unanswered objections or claims without a visible proof path.

### Competitive whitespace

Find an evidence-backed persona, context, angle, proof mechanism, creator representation, or format that customers value but direct competitors do not saturate.

For every gap, cite evidence, state confidence, disclose what was not observable, and propose one falsifiable first test.
