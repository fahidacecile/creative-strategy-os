# Quick Creative Audit

Run a focused, evidence-led Creative Strategy diagnostic. The goal is not to grade taste or copy competitors. The goal is to find the highest-value audience and creative opportunities visible between customer reality, category context, and what the brand currently runs.

## Non-negotiables

- Use independent customer evidence for customer conclusions. Keep brand-owned testimonials separate.
- Every ad claim must come from an ad actually inspected during this run.
- A public ad proves visibility, not spend, performance, profitability, or strategic priority.
- Use `not observed in the visible sample`, never `does not exist`.
- Frame every gap as an opportunity, not an attack on the brand.
- Rank the persona gap first when evidence shows a real buyer or use case that current ads do not represent.
- Treat percentages as estimates from the visible sample and show the sample size.
- Do not invent CTR, CPA, ROAS, revenue, audience size, or expected uplift.
- If a source is blocked, document the limitation and continue only with accessible evidence.

## Intake

Collect:

- Brand and URL
- Hero product, if any
- Market and output language
- Priority paid channel
- User-supplied competitors, if any
- Available first-party performance files, if any
- Desired output: concise report, working document, or presentation-ready narrative

Minimum input: brand URL, market, and language. Detect five direct competitors automatically unless the user supplies a list.

## Workflow

### 1. Build the evidence base

Read [evidence-and-diagnosis.md](references/evidence-and-diagnosis.md). Produce:

- Reputation and brand-context scan
- Independent review and customer-language synthesis
- Three to five evidence-backed personas or jobs-to-be-done
- Visible ad sample for the brand
- Direct-competitor and category scan
- Source register, limitations, and contradiction log

Inspect active ads and ads stopped within the last 90 days when accessible. Analyze up to 100 ads; if more exist, use a balanced sample across status, age, persona, angle, and format.

### 2. Diagnose the gaps

Compare who buys and what customers say with the people, awareness states, angles, proof, creators, and formats visible in current ads.

Diagnose:

1. Persona gap
2. Awareness gap
3. Creative-diversity gap
4. Proof or objection gap when material
5. Competitive whitespace

For each gap, provide an opportunity statement, evidence IDs, current state, missing state, business logic, confidence, and first test.

### 3. Score only what is observable

Read [scoring-and-roadmap.md](references/scoring-and-roadmap.md). Use the Andromeda-aligned diversity score as a secondary diagnostic for the diversity gap, not as the audit's thesis.

If platform-native similarity data is unavailable, mark similarity `not measured`; do not fabricate or normalize an incomplete score to 100.

### 4. Recommend gap-led concepts

Create three to five concepts. Each closes one named gap and includes:

- Target persona
- Awareness state and funnel job
- Strategic angle
- Creator representation or production approach
- Hook direction
- Proof path
- Expected learning
- Evidence IDs

Expected performance or impact remains a hypothesis unless first-party data supports it.

### 5. Deliver the audit

Use [audit-report.md](templates/audit-report.md) and include:

- Executive diagnosis
- Method, scope, sources, and limitations
- Customer-language and persona evidence
- Visible ad-account reality
- Persona, awareness, diversity, proof, and whitespace gaps
- Direct-competitor comparison
- Priority concepts
- 30-, 60-, and 90-day roadmap
- Scorecards and measurement plan
- Evidence-to-recommendation traceability

## Completion gate

The audit is complete only when:

- Customer conclusions cite independent evidence.
- Ads were actually inspected and dated.
- The target brand and five direct competitors are clearly distinguished.
- Sample sizes and inaccessible sources are disclosed.
- The persona gap compares real buyer signals with visible representation.
- The awareness mix and diversity snapshot are reproducible from the sample.
- Every recommendation closes a named gap.
- No result, spend, or performance claim was fabricated.
