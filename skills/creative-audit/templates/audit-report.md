# Creative Audit report template

## 1. Executive diagnosis

- Highest-value opportunity
- Strongest supporting evidence
- Fastest credible test
- Largest evidence limitation

## 2. Method and scope

- Brand, product, market, language, channel
- Observation window
- Sources inspected
- Customer sample size
- Brand ad sample size
- Competitor sample size
- Inaccessible sources and limitations

## 3. Customer and reputation evidence

- Brand and offer context
- Reputation friction
- Customer-language themes
- Golden nuggets with evidence IDs
- Three to five personas or jobs-to-be-done

## 4. Visible ad-account reality

- Personas represented
- Awareness and funnel mix
- Angle, hook, proof, format, and creator distribution
- Partnership signals
- Similarity clusters

## 5. Strategic gaps

For each gap provide:

- Type
- Opportunity statement
- Evidence IDs
- Current state
- Missing state
- Business logic
- Confidence
- First test

## 6. Competitor and whitespace view

- Five direct competitors
- Observable category conventions
- Saturated territories
- Evidence-backed whitespace

## 8. Roadmap

- Days 0-30
- Days 31-60
- Days 61-90

## 9. Scorecards

- Creative strength
- Messaging clarity
- Offer communication
- Funnel coherence
- Evidence and proof
- Andromeda readiness
