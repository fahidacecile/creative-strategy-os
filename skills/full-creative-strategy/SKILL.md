# Full Creative Strategy

Run the complete Creative Strategy OS workflow. The operating rule is:

> Define the objective. Build the evidence base. Diagnose the opportunity. Design a diverse concept system. Brief production. Write copy. Review until approved. Ship a traceable testing plan.

## Non-negotiables

- Evidence before ideation. Do not write concepts or copy until the research synthesis exists.
- Use independent customer evidence for customer claims. Keep brand-owned testimonials separate and label them.
- Never invent reviews, ads, performance, market share, spend, ROAS, CTR, CPA, or inaccessible data.
- Preserve source URLs, observation dates, market, sample size, and confidence for every material finding.
- Separate observation, interpretation, hypothesis, and recommendation.
- One concept equals one primary persona, one awareness state, one strategic angle, one funnel job, and one production format.
- Do not draft copy until the planned batch passes the diversity gate or the user explicitly requests a smaller exploratory set.
- Do not approve final copy until all quality checks pass. Never weaken a claim review to reach the threshold.
- Treat platform-specific guidance as a current hypothesis unless verified from an authoritative, current source.

## Intake

Collect or infer only what can be verified:

- Brand name and URL
- Hero product or offer
- Market and language
- Business objective and marketing objective
- Paid channels
- Target segments, if already known
- Deliverables and deadline
- Claims, legal, brand, production, budget, and travel/location constraints
- Available first-party performance or research files

Minimum viable input: brand URL, market, and output language. If a missing choice materially changes the strategy, ask one focused question. Otherwise, state the assumption and proceed.

## Workflow

### 1. Define the project

Create a Project Objectives note containing the decision to be made, scope, success criteria, channels, constraints, and evidence available. Do not invent KPI targets.

### 2. Build the Research Masterfile

Read [research-and-evidence.md](references/research-and-evidence.md) and follow its source hierarchy, collection rules, evidence schema, and dynamic sampling logic.

Required research streams:

1. Brand, product, offer, positioning, visual and verbal system
2. Independent customer reviews and conversations
3. Market, category, and direct competitors
4. Visible advertising and creative patterns
5. Social, creator, search, and reputation signals where relevant

Output a source register, evidence table, contradiction log, customer-language bank, and research synthesis before continuing.

### 3. Diagnose the strategy

Read [strategy-and-concepts.md](references/strategy-and-concepts.md). Build:

- Brand and market diagnosis
- Three to five evidence-backed personas or jobs-to-be-done
- Awareness-stage map
- Pain, desire, motivation, objection, trigger, and proof maps
- Market sophistication assessment
- Direct-competitor benchmark
- Persona, awareness, creative-diversity, proof, and competitive-whitespace gaps

Rank opportunities by evidence strength, audience relevance, differentiation, production feasibility, and expected learning value. Expected business impact is a hypothesis unless first-party data supports it.

### 4. Create territories, concepts, and the creative grid

Develop distinct creative territories from the diagnosis. Then build a concept matrix across:

- Strategic angle
- Persona
- Awareness state
- Funnel job
- Format
- Hook archetype
- Proof mechanism

Create at least 10 concepts for a full batch unless the user requests another scope. Each concept must differ meaningfully from the others, not only by headline, color, or crop.

Run the Andromeda-aligned diversity gate in [strategy-and-concepts.md](references/strategy-and-concepts.md). If platform-native similarity data is unavailable, mark that dimension `not measured`; do not fabricate it.

### 5. Produce briefs and copy

Read [briefs-copy-and-qc.md](references/briefs-copy-and-qc.md). Build a Brand Spec Card and Copy Masterfile first. Then produce the requested mix of:

- New-concept briefs
- Iteration briefs tied to a real winning creative
- Creator-ready UGC briefs
- Static, video, carousel, or platform-native copy
- Meta Copy Pack where requested

Use [creative-brief.md](templates/creative-brief.md) for each production brief.

### 6. Review and revise

Run the eight-part review in [briefs-copy-and-qc.md](references/briefs-copy-and-qc.md). Reviewers 1-7 must each score at least 90/100. Reviewer 8 is a mechanical copy-edit veto and must return PASS.

When a brief fails, correct the failing elements and rerun the complete review. Preserve elements that already passed unless a change makes them inconsistent.

### 7. Deliver the system

Use [final-deliverables.md](templates/final-deliverables.md). Package:

1. Project Objectives
2. Research Masterfile and source register
3. Brand and market diagnosis
4. Customer-language, persona, awareness, objection, and proof maps
5. Competitor benchmark and creative-gap diagnosis
6. Prioritized territories and creative grid
7. Production-ready briefs and copy
8. Diversity-gate and quality-control reports
9. Creative testing roadmap with hypotheses, variables, metrics, and learning agenda
10. Evidence-to-recommendation traceability table

## Completion gate

The strategy is complete only when:

- Material customer insights point to independent evidence.
- Competitor and ad observations were actually observed and dated.
- Contradictions and limitations are visible.
- Recommendations link back to evidence.
- Concepts are genuinely distinct.
- Claims have a proof path.
- Briefs are executable by a creator or designer without guessing.
- Final copy passes the full quality review.
- The testing roadmap explains what the team should learn, not only what it should launch.
