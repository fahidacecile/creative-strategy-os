# Strategy, gaps, concepts, and diversity gate

Use this reference after the Research Masterfile is complete.

## Persona model

Build three to five evidence-backed personas or jobs-to-be-done. A useful persona is defined by buying context and psychology, not decorative demographics.

For each persona, document:

- Situation and job-to-be-done
- Desired progress
- Pain and emotional tension
- Trigger and buying moment
- Objections and risk perception
- Proof required
- Awareness state
- Customer-language excerpts and evidence IDs
- Evidence volume, intensity, contradictions, and confidence

Do not merge conflicting motivations merely to reduce the number of personas.

## Awareness map

Use five customer awareness states:

1. Unaware
2. Problem aware
3. Solution aware
4. Product aware
5. Most aware

Keep awareness separate from funnel position. Awareness describes what the buyer understands; TOFU, MOFU, and BOFU describe the buyer's relationship with this brand's funnel.

## Gap diagnosis

Evaluate these gaps in order:

### Persona gap

Compare people visible in customer evidence with people represented or addressed by current ads. Frame the gap as a new audience opportunity, not a criticism. Require at least one supporting evidence cluster and state uncertainty.

### Awareness gap

Estimate the visible creative mix by awareness state and funnel job. Identify an over-index and the missing job needed to keep acquisition, consideration, and conversion connected.

### Creative-diversity gap

Assess angle range, hook range, format mix, creator representation, partnership signals, proof types, and similarity clusters. Distinguish meaningful conceptual diversity from cosmetic variants.

### Proof or objection gap

Use when customers repeatedly express an objection that visible ads do not answer, or when advertising claims exceed available proof.

### Competitive whitespace

Identify a persona, angle, proof mechanism, context, or creative territory that is relevant to customers but not saturated across direct competitors.

For every gap provide: opportunity statement, evidence IDs, current state, missing state, why it matters, confidence, and the first test.

## Creative territories

Turn the priority gaps into three to six territories. Each territory needs:

- Human tension
- Strategic angle
- Target persona and awareness state
- Promise and proof path
- Emotional register
- Brand fit
- Competitive distinction
- Suitable formats and creators
- Claim boundary

Reject territories that are unsupported, interchangeable, or merely format labels.

## Concept matrix

Use the formula:

`persona x awareness x funnel job x angle x format x proof = distinct concept`

Create at least 10 concepts for a full batch. Apply these rules:

- Each concept differs from every other concept on at least two meaningful dimensions.
- No angle, persona, format, awareness state, or hook archetype should dominate without a stated strategic reason.
- A headline rewrite, crop, color swap, or creator swap with an unchanged idea is an iteration, not a new concept.
- Test strategic angle before minor execution variables when there is no established winner.
- If reliable performance data exists, use a 60/40 planning split: about 60% disciplined iterations on validated winners and 40% net-new angles. Without a validated winner, label the batch exploratory instead of inventing one.

## Andromeda-aligned diversity gate

This is a planning heuristic, not an official Meta guarantee. Cite current authoritative platform information when making platform claims.

| Dimension | Points | Scoring |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Concept volume | 15 | 15 for 12+; 10 for 10-11; 5 for 7-9; 0 below 7 |
| Creative similarity | 20 | Use platform-native similarity data when available. If unavailable, mark `not measured` and add a qualitative duplicate review. Do not invent a score. |
| Hook archetype diversity | 20 | 20 for 4+; 12 for 3; 5 for 2; 0 for 1 |
| Format mix | 20 | 20 for strategically justified static, video, and carousel coverage; 12 for two strong formats; 5 for a narrow mix; 0 for one repeated format |
| Persona coverage | 15 | 15 when each priority persona has 2+ concepts; 10 when all are covered; 5 when one persona exceeds half the batch; 0 for one persona only |
| Angle and funnel spread | 10 | 10 for 3+ angles and all necessary funnel jobs; 6 for partial spread; 3 for one dominant angle; 0 for a single angle and job |

If every dimension is measurable, use these thresholds:

- 75-100: approved for briefing
- 50-74: revise the weakest dimensions
- 0-49: rebuild the matrix

If a dimension is not measurable, report both the measured subtotal and `provisional, incomplete score`; do not normalize it to 100.

## Prioritization

Score each concept 1-5 on:

- Evidence strength
- Persona relevance
- Differentiation
- Proof availability
- Production feasibility
- Expected learning value
- Brand fit

Do not present expected performance as fact. The output is a prioritized testing hypothesis.
