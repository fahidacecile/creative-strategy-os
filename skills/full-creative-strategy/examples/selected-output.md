# Selected output: fictional illustration

This is a deliberately small illustration of output structure, not a completed strategy and not evidence about a real brand.

## Example insight

**Observation:** Category conversations repeatedly separate everyday hydration from endurance use. `[CAT-014, CAT-039, CAT-071]`

**Interpretation:** A daily-routine buyer may require a different context, proof standard, and creator than a performance athlete.

**Hypothesis:** A routine-led territory could expand relevance beyond sports without making health-performance claims.

**Confidence:** Medium. Category evidence is available, but the fictional brand has no customer or performance data.

## Example persona

**The Meeting-to-Meeting Professional**

- Job: maintain a simple hydration habit during a desk-heavy day
- Tension: remembers water only when energy or concentration already feels low
- Desired progress: an easy ritual that fits beside coffee and calls
- Proof required: taste, portability, transparent ingredients, and credible category explanation
- Awareness: solution aware
- Claim boundary: no claim that the product treats fatigue or improves cognition

## Example concept

**Concept ID:** C-04

- Persona: Meeting-to-Meeting Professional
- Awareness: Solution aware
- Funnel job: TOFU acquisition
- Angle: hydration as a visible desk ritual
- Format: 20-second creator desk-reset video
- Hook: "My water reminder became part of the clutter."
- Proof path: sachet portability, preparation demonstration, ingredient transparency
- Hypothesis: a recognizable workday moment will earn more qualified attention than sports-performance framing
