# Sample input: fictional brand

This example is fictional and contains no client data.

```text
Use Full Creative Strategy for Northstar Hydration, a fictional electrolyte powder.

Market: United States
Language: English
Hero product: Daily Hydration Sachets
Channels: Meta and TikTok
Objective: Build a first paid-social creative testing plan for customer acquisition.
Constraints: No medical claims. Use only public independent customer evidence from the electrolyte category. Clearly separate category evidence from brand-specific evidence because the brand is fictional.
Deliverables: Research synthesis, personas, awareness map, competitor benchmark, creative gaps, 10 concepts, 3 production-ready briefs, draft copy, QC report, and 90-day testing roadmap.
```

Because the brand is fictional, the agent must not fabricate brand reviews, live ads, performance, or customers. Category evidence may support hypotheses, not brand-specific claims.
