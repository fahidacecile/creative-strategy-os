# Full Creative Strategy delivery template

## 1. Executive decision brief

- Objective
- Highest-value customer opportunity
- Primary creative gap
- Recommended strategic move
- First test
- Material limitation

## 2. Research Masterfile

- Scope and source register
- Brand and product truth
- Independent customer evidence
- Customer-language bank
- Market and competitor evidence
- Advertising sample
- Contradictions and limitations

## 3. Strategic diagnosis

- Personas or jobs-to-be-done
- Awareness and funnel map
- Pain, desire, motivation, objection, trigger, and proof maps
- Market sophistication
- Competitor benchmark
- Persona, awareness, diversity, proof, and whitespace gaps

## 4. Creative system

- Priority territories
- Concept matrix
- Diversity-gate report
- Concept prioritization

## 5. Production pack

- Brand Spec Card
- Copy Masterfile
- Creative briefs
- Scripts and copy
- Meta Copy Pack, if requested
- Quality-control report

## 6. Testing roadmap

For each test include concept ID, hypothesis, variable, control, audience, placement, metric, decision rule, owner, launch window, and learning.

## 7. Traceability

| Recommendation | Evidence IDs | Interpretation | Confidence | Test or validation step |
